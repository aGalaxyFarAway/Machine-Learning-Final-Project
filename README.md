Depending on the industry, the difference between a good and bad classfication can be the difference between life and death. With this in mind, our group used the ["Personal Key Indicators of Heart Disease"](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kamilpytlak/personal-key-indicators-of-heart-disease/data) dataset to determine what hyperparameters would optimize the perceptron, RandomForest, and multi-layered perceptron (MLP) models to give the fewest false-negatives (i.e. maximize recall). By maximizing the recall, the machine-learning model will be tuned to misclassify patients as not having heart-disease when they actually do. Further, after hyper-parameter tuning, we determined the best model to be the perceptron after comparison of exhaustive statistical tests across the three chosen machine learning models.
